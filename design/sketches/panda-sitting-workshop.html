Export & bake

How this bakes in. The export is a SIT_DIRS object — the five drawn facings (up, dUp, side, dDown, down), each with its wobble frames in play order, plus the grid, the play order, and the left-facing mirror map. In pandas.js a rider picks its facing from the base's heading exactly like the walk sprite (DIR_SPRITE), renders the frame's map as an <svg viewBox="0 0 48 48"> of rects, mirrors with scale(-1,1) for the three left facings, and cycles the wobble from the Stack tick.

base sprite: “panda collision” by masahito (ma5a) — codepen.io/Ma5a/pen/WNEBqPO · MIT; the five walk cels are traced under that license, with thanks. The seated poses, wobble, and this studio are ours — hand-drawn here, never generated. Sibling of design/sketches/panda-behaviors-workshop.html.
